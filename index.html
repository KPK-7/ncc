About NCC at Thapar Polytechnic College

The NCC unit at Thapar Polytechnic College is dedicated to building character, discipline, and leadership among students. Through active participation in drills, camps, and community service, cadets learn patriotism, teamwork, and responsibility. Our ANO and senior cadets guide every member to serve the nation with integrity and honor.
